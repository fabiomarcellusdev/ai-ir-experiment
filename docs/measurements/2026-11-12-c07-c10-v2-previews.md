## What the runs show

1. **Rule C.6.3 does not reopen decision 0008.** The v2 previews show fewer turns in arm M than the v1 previews: 3
   against 4 for C0.7, and 3 against 5 for C0.9.
2. **C0.7 v2 is the strongest result so far.** The median ratio is 0.380, and the upper end of its interval, 0.45, is
   well under both 0.70 and 1.00. Arm B cost $0.59 and arm M $0.07: a baseline agent reads and sends large targets.
   Arm B failed 1 removal (the canonical DOM is not the expected file's); arm M failed none.
3. **C0.9 v2 improved from 1.194 to 0.793,** with equal success. It is still above the gate's 0.70, and its interval
   reaches 1.26. With 6 tasks, this preview cannot say on which side of 0.70 the gate will fall.
4. **C0.10 v2 shows the overhead on small tasks.** Both arms need 3 turns, but each turn of arm M also carries the v2
   tool text. On these tasks the baseline is cheap (median 14,059 tokens), so the ratio is 1.209 with a narrow
   interval. C0.6 gave 1.40 with v1 on the same task kind.
5. **One C0.10 task is ambiguous: `10-text`.** The task asks to change the text "Share on Facebook" in a `span`. The
   same words are also the `aria-label` and the `title` of the button next to it. Both arms changed them too, so both
   failed in the same way. C0.3's text rule checks only the visible text of other elements, not attribute values.
   This is a defect of the task rule, not of an arm (`docs/follow-ups.md`).

## What these numbers do not show

- **The gates' results.** The previews use C0.5's 10 files, 1 run a task. The gates draw new files.
- **C0.8.** Its preview has 4 tasks, under the minimum of 6.
- **The cause of the C0.9 change.** v2's `html_find` returns the attributes with each match, and its locator saves a
  ref step; the previews do not separate these effects.
