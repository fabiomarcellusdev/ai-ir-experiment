# Risk check for gate C0.3: outline only, or outline plus a find tool

**Partial result: proxy token counts on 3 small synthetic files outside the gate's file set. This is not a run of
gate C0.3, and its inputs are not like the gate's files.**

## The question

The prior-art research (`docs/prior-art.md`) points to one premise: a narrow read after a search costs much less than
a whole-file read. Gate C0.3 gives the baseline that path: Grep for the quoted words, then 20 lines before and after.
Is the MCP's ideal path cheaper? Does a find tool change the answer?

## The conditions

- Date: 2026-11-10. NOT timed. Node v24.22.1.
- Code: `ir.mjs` and `bench.mjs` from commit `748a75d` (`exp/html-ir-poc`). Script: `2026-11-10-c03-risk-check/check.mjs`,
  with `tooldefs.mjs` beside it.
- Command, from a folder that holds the script, `tooldefs.mjs`, `ir.mjs` and `bench.mjs`:
  `node check.mjs 50 <samples folder> <pages folder>`. With `RAW=1`, the last output line holds the raw values. The
  raw values of this run are in `2026-11-10-c03-risk-check.csv`.
- Inputs:
  - The POC's 3 samples, from `748a75d:experiments/html-ir/samples/`. They are synthetic, formatted, and 4 KB to
    9 KB. The gate excludes files under 10 KB.
  - The POC's 5 public pages (`748a75d:experiments/html-ir/pages.txt`). All 5 have lines over 2,000 characters
    (github 8, hackernews 1, mdn 9, tailwind 2, wikipedia 121). The gate excludes such files.
  - The gate's file set excludes all 8 files, so this check does not touch the gate's inputs.
- Tokens: proxy A of the POC (a GPT-2-style pre-tokenizer count), not Claude's tokenizer.
- Tasks: 20 targets for each file and seed, drawn from the elements inside `body` with 3 words or more of visible
  text. The seed is new for each file: `20261110` + 0 to 49 (50 seeds). The quote is the first 6 words of the
  target's visible text.
- The paths, each with its arm's tool definitions and its edit payload:
  - **B:** the cheaper of two paths. (1) The whole file through Read: 2,000 lines a call, with line numbers, and lines
    cut at 2,000 characters. (2) When the quote is in a raw line: Grep's output, plus one read of all matched lines
    with 20 lines before and after.
  - **M1:** the whole outline with refs, plus the node.
  - **M2:** a draft `html_find` (the deepest matches; each ancestor line once), plus the node. Valid only when the
    target is among the first 20 matches.
  - **Edit payload:** B sends the old and new text (2 × the quote). M sends the whole new node.
  - **Tool definitions:** B uses the harness's Read, Edit, Grep and Glob definitions (783 proxy tokens; not committed,
    see `tooldefs.mjs`). M uses Grep, Glob and the drafts of the 4 MCP tools (565).
- The node is the canonical node's HTML, not the file's original bytes: the POC has no source positions.

## The numbers

For each file: the median over 50 seeds of the median ratio over 20 tasks, the range over the seeds, and the share of
seeds in which the median is 0.50 or less.

| File | Outline only (M1 ÷ B) | Cheaper of outline and find |
|---|---|---|
| sample: admin form, 6 KB | 0.89 [0.73–1.14], 0% | 0.73 [0.48–0.96], 22% |
| sample: docs article, 4 KB | 0.88 [0.81–1.04], 0% | 0.41 [0.37–0.84], 98% |
| sample: Tailwind landing, 9 KB | 0.49 [0.39–0.64], 54% | 0.31 [0.23–0.42], 100% |

The 5 pages are left out of the conclusion. When Read cuts their long lines, the baseline looks cheap only because it
cannot see the content. Their rows are in the CSV file.

## What the numbers show

- **Outline only:** on 2 of the 3 samples, no seed passes 0.50. With the agreed tools, C0.3 would probably fail on
  formatted files.
- **With a find tool:** 2 of the 3 samples pass in most seeds, and 1 fails in most seeds. C0.3 is uncertain, not
  likely to pass.

## What the numbers do not show

- **The gate's real result.** The samples are small and synthetic. The proxy is not Claude's tokenizer.
- **The effect of real tool definitions.** They are a large part of each path on these small files. The MCP drafts
  are short; longer descriptions raise M. The baseline's definitions can change with the harness version.
- **Larger files.** On a larger formatted file, the Grep path and the find path stay about the same size, and the
  outline grows. So the find path's ratio probably stays near these values, and outline only gets worse. This is a
  reasoned guess, not a measurement.
- **Gate C0.4.** There, the agent's whole fixed text (its system prompt, often thousands of tokens) repeats on every
  turn in both arms. That pulls the ratio toward 1 even more. Not measured.
