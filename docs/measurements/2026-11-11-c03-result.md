## What the numbers show

- **The gate fails.** The median is 0.739 against 0.50. Only 2 of 40 tasks are at 0.50 or less, and 3 quarters of
  the tasks are above 0.639. No task type is below 0.68.
- **The real count is worse for the MCP than the preview.** The median rose from about 0.65 (proxies) to 0.739.
  The preflight showed why: the proxies under-counted the find path more than the Grep path.
- **Against a Grep-capable agent, the MCP saves about a quarter** (its find path against Grep plus a 41-line read).
- **The outline alone costs about 2 times the Grep path.**
- **Against a whole-file read, the find path saves about 84%** (median of the find path ÷ the whole-file path: 0.165).
  The gate does not measure this: its baseline takes its cheapest path.

## What the numbers do not show

- **What a real agent does.** The paths are ideal. A real agent may read more than 41 lines, or the whole file. That
  is gate C0.4, which the contract runs only after C0.3 passes.
- **Other models.** Only `claude-sonnet-5-5` was counted.
