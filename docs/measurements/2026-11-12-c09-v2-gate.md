## The stop

The gate stopped once, with no verdict, and went on at the same commit, as the contract's stop rule says (amendment
of 2026-11-12, C0.6 rule 2).

1. At 21:25 the lead committed the C0.7 report (`bfae0ae`) while the gate ran. The runner's check found that HEAD had
   changed, and stopped the gate after 58 records. The lead's commit was a mistake.
2. Run 59 (`9-text`, arm B, run 1) had ended its model part normally (5 turns, $0.0117), but the judge had not run.
   The check throws an error of a kind that writes no stop line, so the run had no outcome. At 21:29 the runner
   refused to go on until the owner decided; it wrote nothing and ran nothing.
3. The owner chose to go on at `540f2d4` (2026-11-12). The lead checked out `540f2d4` with a detached HEAD.
4. The owner chose to mark run 59 as stopped. The lead appended one `stopped` line to the progress file, in the shape
   that the runner writes, with the run's cost.
5. The gate went on at 21:32 and ran run 59 again and the 181 other runs. The result lists the stop.

The stopped attempt's outcome was never seen, so the choice to run it again cannot favour an arm. If that attempt had
been the record, the task's ratio would be 0.7045, not 0.7036, and the gate's median would not change.

The contract's stop rule names "an API or harness error". A stop by the runner's HEAD check is not named
(`docs/follow-ups.md`).

## Margin and noise

These numbers are from the `analysis-checker` agent. They are not gate checks.

1. **Tasks near the limit:** 22 of 40 tasks are at or below 0.70. 12 tasks are between 0.65 and 0.75.
2. **What would change the verdict:** 3 tasks that move from under 0.70 to over it, or a rise of 1.39% in all of arm
   M's tokens (about 240 tokens a run).
3. **Each round alone:** 0.692, 0.687 and 0.693. All 3 are at or below 0.70.
4. **Without each file in turn:** the median goes from 0.680 to 0.698. No single file moves it above 0.70.
5. **The bootstrap:** 26% of the resamples over files have a median above 0.70. A bootstrap of the 3 runs inside each
   task (not in the contract) gives 0.670 to 0.705, with 9.7% of resamples above 0.70.

## What these numbers do not show

- **That the ratio is reliably 0.70 or less.** The margin is smaller than the spread between rounds and between
  files. Another draw of 10 files could fall on either side.
- **Structure changes.** Their median ratio is 0.873: v2 saves less on them than on the other types.
- **Other models or harnesses.** One model and one harness version ran.
- **Real work.** The tasks are single edits on single files, with a prompt that names the target.
- **The cost with deferred tool loading.** The tool text was in every turn of arm M (`ENABLE_TOOL_SEARCH=false`).
